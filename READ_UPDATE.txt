Replace these four files in the SAME GitHub repository, keeping the other app files. Back up first and reopen while online. Do not clear website data.

Highlight replaces Mark. Scroll and Text highlight are hidden. Tap the selected Pen or Highlight again to scroll normally. Press-and-hold copy/look-up is suppressed on reading pages. Pen is thinner. Drawing defaults to stylus-only input; finger/palm touches do not draw when your stylus is recognized as a pen.

For a universal stylus that behaves like a finger, enable More > Finger / universal pen. In that mode the browser cannot distinguish your palm from the stylus. Keep your palm lifted or use a drawing glove.

Notes (top right) lists saved notes and bookmarks. Tap Bookmark to save the current page. Open Notes and tap its page number to return to a bookmark.
